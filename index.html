AI is how I engineer, not a buzzword

One engineer can own a whole platform when the process is right. Mine runs on AI agents with clear context, rules and verification.

  • Agent-driven development

    Layered project context, per-area rules, MCP integrations and subagents. Plans live next to the code and nothing ships before it's verified in production.

  • AI inside the products

    The apps I build include AI chatbots, operations-analysis agents and neural text-to-speech voices for emergency alerts.

  • Cost and quality aware

    I model LLM cost per conversation, pick the right model for each job and review every change an agent makes before it reaches users.
